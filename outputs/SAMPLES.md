# Instrument sample credits

Piano, acoustic guitar, violin, flute, and clarinet recordings are loaded from [ToneJS Instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments). Its README identifies the sample files as CC BY 3.0; source notes are in the repository's [`sample-source-info.txt`](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/master/sample-source-info.txt). The original samples are pitch-shifted to the note assigned to each control.

The kick, snare, and hi-hat recordings are from [ToneJS Audio's acoustic kit](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/drum-samples/acoustic-kit), which identifies [cwilso/web-audio-samples](https://github.com/cwilso/web-audio-samples) as the sample source.

The three toms and crash/ride cymbals are from [octalmusic/octal-samples](https://github.com/octalmusic/octal-samples), dedicated CC0-1.0. Its README describes the tom sounds as generated and the cymbals as processed derivatives of public-domain uzu-drumkit material.
