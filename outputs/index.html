How to play

01
Choose an instrument

Free Play starts as soon as you choose. Rhythm Mode and Song Library are optional tabs if you want a song.

02
Play the instrument

Tap or click its keys, strings, frets, or drum pads. Open the computer keyboard guide for mapped keys. Hold multiple controls together where the instrument supports chords.

03
Play with sound and timing

Use the volume slider and Stop sound control. In Rhythm Mode, press the instrument’s matching controls as notes reach the target line; Space pauses or resumes.
